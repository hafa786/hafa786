# 💫 About Me:
I build software that turns complex problems into scalable, production-ready products — from cloud-native platforms to AI-powered applications.<br><br>I’m a Senior Software Engineer with 9+ years of experience across backend, full-stack, cloud, and AI engineering. I enjoy owning challenging problems end to end: shaping architecture, writing production code, deploying infrastructure, and improving systems after they go live. My core stack includes Java/Spring Boot, Python, Go, React, TypeScript, AWS, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, microservices, and PostgreSQL.<br><br>More recently, I’ve been building intelligent products with Generative AI, LLMs, AI agents, and MCP. I currently lead the technical development of an AI-powered health assistant, taking ownership from architecture through production deployment. Previously, I worked on biomedical and genomics software at the University of Helsinki/FIMM and helped build a centralized identity and access management platform at Blueprint Genetics. Across my career, I’ve worked in healthcare, genomics, video technology, SaaS, and cloud-native environments.<br><br>I’m especially interested in what happens when strong software engineering meets practical AI. I’m looking to build systems where AI delivers measurable value rather than simply being added as a feature — while continuing to solve hard problems around architecture, scalability, APIs, distributed systems, and cloud infrastructure.<br><br>Always happy to connect with people building ambitious software and AI products.
